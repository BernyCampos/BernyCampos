### About me

I'm **Berny**, a developer who enjoys combining thoughtful interface design with practical engineering. I build customizable **Pelican Panel** experiences, work on **Discord** integrations and music systems, and explore **Minecraft** tooling and self-hosted infrastructure.

My goal is simple: make complex technology feel polished, approachable, and reliable.
